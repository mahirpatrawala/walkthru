walkthru family bodies (SPEC 16.6.7, built 13 Nov 2026)

Files: person-man.glb, person-woman.glb, person-elder.glb, person-child.glb and their -low.glb versions, thumb-*.jpg, clips.glb
Every file here is released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

What they were made from
- MakeHuman base mesh (hm08) and macro targets (age, gender, weight), shaped with MPFB 2.0.18 in Blender 5.3 and given MPFB's "game_engine"
  rig and its skin weights. MakeHuman's licence page "What changed regarding the license in 2020"
  (https://static.makehumancommunity.org/oldsite/faq/what_changed_regarding_the_license_in_2020.html) says base mesh, targets and proxies are
  graphical assets covered by CC0 ("Targets are CC0 no matter how you got hold of them"), and the FAQ "Are MakeHuman files free?"
  (https://static.makehumancommunity.org/makehuman/faq/are_makehuman_files_free.html) gives the asset licence as CC0.
  MPFB2 itself (GPL-3.0-or-later) is a tool here: no MPFB code ships.
- Three MakeHuman system assets, each file carries the header "This asset was explicitly released as CC0 in september 2020"
  (Copyright 2020 Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier): eyes/high-poly, eyebrows/eyebrow001, clothes/shoes05.
  Their textures are not used: every part is a flat colour set at run time.
- Garments (kurta, pyjama, kameez, salwar, blouse, saree, dupatta, pallu, trim), hair, bun, braid, pigtails and pupils were modelled
  for walkthru by tools/make_family.py from the shape of the skin itself. They contain no third-party asset and are CC0 with the rest.
- The six animation clips in clips.glb are the Universal Animation Library clips Idle_Loop, Walk_Loop, Jog_Fwd_Loop, Sitting_Enter,
  Sitting_Idle_Loop, Sitting_Exit by Quaternius (CC0, https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html), taken from
  public/characters/mannequin.glb and retargeted onto these bodies when the page loads. Look_Around and Reach_Forward are made at load time from them.
