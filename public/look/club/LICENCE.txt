Clubhouse assets under public/look/club/ (SPEC 16.17.3). Fetched and checked by scripts/fetch-club-assets.ts; club.lock.json has every source URL, size, MD5 and SHA-256.
The files here are our own conversions (tools/optimise-club-assets.mjs): a model is cut down to the parts used, decimated, re-centred, re-surfaced with the app's own materials where the source carried print or wear, and meshopt-compressed.
