Gym assets under public/look/gym/ (SPEC 16.17.1). Fetched and checked by scripts/fetch-gym-assets.ts; gym.lock.json has every source URL, size, MD5 and SHA-256.
The files here are our own conversions (tools/optimise-gym-assets.mjs): a model is cut down to the one object used, decimated, re-centred, split into parts that take the app's own materials, and meshopt-compressed; a map is resized and re-encoded.
