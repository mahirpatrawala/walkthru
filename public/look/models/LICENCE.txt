Sources looked at for better furniture models on 16 Nov 2026. Nothing from any of them is shipped: no CC0 model that fits the catalogue sizes and looks better than the generated piece exists, and the free hosted 3D generators allow about one model a day without an account. The generated furniture (src/look/gen) was improved instead.

  Poly Haven models, re-checked
    https://api.polyhaven.com/assets?t=models
    licence: CC0 1.0, https://polyhaven.com/license: "Our assets are all licensed as CC0"
    result: 522 models listed, 159 furniture, seating, table, bed, shelf, appliance, electronics or lighting pieces, none added since the library was built that fits a catalogue size within 12 percent and is a clean contemporary piece. chinese_sofa (2.29 x 0.97 x 0.86 m) fits the 3-seat sofa but is a carved Chinese-style piece; modern_arm_chair_01 (0.82 x 0.99 x 1.02 m) fits only the recliner box and is not a recliner; the day beds, bed frames and dining_table of the Lighthouse collection are weathered or the wrong height.
  Objaverse 1.0 (Sketchfab) CC0 subset
    https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse
    licence: per object; Sketchfab licence slug 'cc0' in the dataset metadata, re-checkable at https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/<uid>
    result: 3465 of the 800k objects are CC0; the 67 with a furniture-like name are museum scans (Smithsonian, Cleveland, Polish museums: antique chairs, chests, lamps). No modern sofa, bed, wardrobe, commode, basin, fridge or washing machine.

  TRELLIS (Microsoft) image-to-3D, hosted Space trellis-community/TRELLIS and microsoft/TRELLIS.2
    https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS
    licence: README: "TRELLIS models and the majority of the code are licensed under the MIT License. The following submodules may have different licenses: diffoctreerast ... Modified Flexicubes"; weights TRELLIS-image-large and TRELLIS.2-4B: license mit (model cards)
    result: NOT USED. The texture baking of TRELLIS runs the Inria gaussian rasteriser (diffoctreerast, https://github.com/JeffreyXiang/diffoctreerast/blob/master/LICENSE: "The Software may be used non-commercially, i.e., for research and/or evaluation purposes only") and nvdiffrast (https://github.com/NVlabs/nvdiffrast/blob/main/LICENSE.txt: "Nvidia Source Code License (1-Way Commercial)"). TRELLIS.2 conditions on DINOv3 (facebook/dinov3-vitl16-pretrain-lvd1689m, Meta's own licence) and removes backgrounds with briaai/RMBG-2.0 (non-commercial). So the generator's stack is not MIT or Apache end to end, which the owner's rule asks for.
