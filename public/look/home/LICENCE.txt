Models of the flats under public/look/home/ (SPEC 16.15.4). Fetched and checked by scripts/fetch-home-models.ts; home.lock.json has every source URL, size and SHA-256.
The files here are adaptations (tools/optimise-home-models.mjs): turned to face the room, scaled to metres, re-centred, cut to the parts used, simplified, some parts given plain materials, maps re-encoded as WebP, meshopt-compressed. In the app each is further scaled to the drawn size and recoloured.
